## Geographic data

`public/textures/earth-water.png` and `native/NetLens.Core/countries.json` are generated by `tools/make-land-data.mjs` from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) data (public domain), using the [world-atlas](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) package (ISC). Country codes come from [i18n-iso-countries](https://github.com/michaelwittig/node-i18n-iso-countries) (MIT). Country borders and names aren't a statement about any territorial claim.
